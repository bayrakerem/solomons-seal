Delete your account and data

Solomon's Seal (developer: Kerem Emre Bayrak) creates an anonymous player account when you first open the game. It stores your nickname, avatar, level, stars, league entry and friend list. No email, name or phone number is collected.

In the game (instant)

Open Profile → Settings → Delete my account. Your account, league entry, friend list and progress are removed from our server immediately.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, send a request below. Your player ID is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen (8 characters); your friend code is on the Profile screen. Either one is enough.





What is deleted, and when

Everything linked to the account is deleted within 30 days of a web request: account record, nickname, avatar, progress, league entry and friend list. Nothing is kept. Purchase records are held by Apple / Google, not by us; ad data is handled by Google AdMob under its own policy.

Hesabını ve verilerini sil

Oyunda (anında): Profil → Ayarlar → Hesabımı sil.

Oyun yüklü değilse: yukarıdaki forma oyuncu kimliğini (Ayarlar ekranının altında, 8 karakter) ya da arkadaş kodunu yaz. Hesabına bağlı her şey (kayıt, takma ad, ilerleme, lig, arkadaş listesi) 30 gün içinde silinir.
